Policy & Disclaimer

Safeguarding clarity for South African students

Purpose

This app and website exist to help students navigate bursary opportunities and access prospectuses from all visible universities in South Africa. Our goal is to provide clear, accessible information — not to replace official university communication.

Subscribe to Assist Channel

Support this project by subscribing to our assist channel. Enter your email below to help us grow and stay updated.

Disclaimer

  • We are not an official bursary provider or university representative.
  • Information is provided for educational purposes only.
  • Always confirm bursary deadlines and requirements directly with the university or funding body.

Usage Policy

  • Users may freely access and share links to this site.
  • Content must not be copied, redistributed, or altered without permission.
  • Prospectuses linked here remain the property of their respective universities.
  • Videos Generated are Made by Lebogang Prince Kula (@honorableking99).
  • Check for South African Universities & Prospectuses section on this policy for mor clarity

Privacy

This site does not collect personal data beyond standard analytics. Students are encouraged to review university privacy policies when submitting applications.

Student Guidance

Not all videos or external resources explain bursaries clearly. For clarity, always start here — then feel free to check other videos or university pages for more detail.
